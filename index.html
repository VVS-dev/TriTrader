How it works

Four steps from sign-up to the final ranking

Everyone starts with the same simulated account and trades the same markets for the same week, so the ranking comes down to your trading.

1

Sign up and verify

Create an account, confirm your email, then complete a quick ID check with a selfie. It's one person, one account, and you must be 18 or over.

2

Enter and pick your leverage

Pay the £100 entry fee and choose leverage from 1:1 to 1:500 (1:100 by default). Your leverage stays fixed for that entry.

3

Trade for a week

Monday 00:01 to Sunday 23:59 UK. Use market, limit, stop and stop-limit orders, with take-profit, stop-loss, trailing stops and hedging.

4

Final ranking and prizes

Traders are ranked by return %. After a fair-play review the results are final, and prizes are paid by bank transfer to your verified name.

Fair play

Fair fills, real people, results you can check

A competition only means something if everyone plays by the same rules. The rules below apply to everyone.

Fair fills

Every order waits a random 100–300 ms, then fills on the next live price. Nobody can trade on a price that's already gone.

No bots

Automated trading is banned and there's no trading API. Trading happens in the browser only, and it's monitored for automation.

One verified person, one account

Every trader passes an ID and liveness check before entering. Duplicate identities are blocked.

Every trade is replayable

Every price and fill is recorded in sequence, so any result can be replayed and checked.

Public leaderboard

Rankings are public and refresh every 30 seconds. Open positions are shown 15 minutes delayed, under trader handles, never real names.

Reviewed before payment

Every prize winner is reviewed before payout, and final results and payouts are published.

FAQ

Questions traders ask first

Is any real trading involved?

No. All trading on TriTrader is simulated. No real orders are placed, and you never own or trade any financial instrument. Prices come from live third-party sources. Your simulated balance has no cash value and can't be withdrawn.

What's the most I can lose?

The most you can lose is the entry fees you pay. A blown account can't be topped up; you can re-enter up to twice, until Thursday 17:00 UK.

How are winners decided?

By return % on your simulated account at the end of the week. Only your best entry counts. Ties go to the trader who reached that return first. Results are provisional until the fair-play review is complete, then final.

Where do the prizes come from?

TriTrader sets and pays them. The prizes are fixed and published before entries open, and they don't change with the number of entries or re-entries.

Who can enter?

Anyone aged 18 or over who passes identity verification, from any country except those under UK sanctions. It's one person, one account.

How are prizes paid?

By bank transfer to an account in your verified name, once the results are final.

Is this a prop firm challenge?

No. There's no evaluation and no funded account at the end. You pay for one entry into a week-long competition, and fixed prizes go to the top-ranked traders.

What does joining the waitlist do?

We'll email you when entries open for the Beta Championship. It's free, and there's no payment now.

Risk disclosure

Before you enter

  1. Simulated trading. All trading on TriTrader is simulated. No real orders are placed, and you never own or trade any financial instrument. Prices come from third-party sources and may differ from other platforms.
  2. Leverage. Trading on margin multiplies gains and losses. With leverage of 1:100, a price move of 1% against a position equals the whole margin for that position. Your simulated account can lose its entire balance quickly.
  3. Margin call and stop-out. Your margin level is equity divided by used margin. At 100% you get a margin call warning. At 50% the platform automatically closes your positions, largest loss first, until your margin level is back above 50%. Stop-outs can fill at worse prices than you expect.
  4. Your maximum loss. The most you can lose is the entry fees you pay. A blown account can't be topped up; you can re-enter up to twice, until Thursday 17:00 UK.
  5. Not advice. Nothing on TriTrader is investment advice. Competition results don't predict results in real markets.
